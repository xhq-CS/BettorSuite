# v2.7.0 — Public Launch & Trust Layer

> BettorSuite has a real front door now—and a proper lock.

This release turns the signed-out experience into a polished product preview and adds the account-security, safety, and policy foundation expected from a public community.

## Highlights

- **A landing page with a pulse.** Visitors can understand Book Keeper, Mock Betting, Daily Cards, profiles, and community features before they create an account.
- **Recovery that does not leak account data.** Password-reset requests use generic responses, one-time 30-minute tokens, hashed storage, and automatic session revocation after a successful reset.
- **A proper Security center.** Members can change their password, inspect active sessions, revoke individual devices, or sign out everywhere else.
- **Block and report controls.** Blocking now changes real server behavior across profiles, discovery, follows, DMs, groups, the War Room, and shared content.
- **A moderation safety inbox.** Administrators can review reports and privacy requests without mixing them into routine account tools.
- **Public policy surfaces.** Privacy, Terms, Community Guidelines, Responsible Gambling, and privacy-request pages are available without signing in.
- **Launch-ready sharing.** BettorSuite now includes dedicated Open Graph artwork, social metadata, and public indexing rules.

## Under the hood

- Added schema coverage for reset tokens, policy consent timestamps, session metadata, user blocks, safety reports, and privacy requests.
- Added `lib/db/migrations/v2.7.0.sql` for existing databases.
- Added dedicated limits and ownership checks around sensitive account and safety operations.
- Kept the project free of live sports-data dependencies; betting records remain user-entered and user-owned.

## Before production

1. Apply the v2.7.0 database migration.
2. Set `RESEND_API_KEY` and a verified `EMAIL_FROM` sender in Vercel.
3. Confirm `APP_ORIGIN` matches the canonical production domain.
4. Verify login, sign-up, recovery, legal pages, block/report flows, and the administrator safety inbox.
5. Have qualified counsel review the policy templates against the final operator entity, jurisdiction, audience, and business practices.

## Compatibility

This is a backward-compatible feature release. Existing accounts, sessions, betting history, wallets, groups, and messages remain intact.
